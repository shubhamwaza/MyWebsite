02 / 18 · PROBLEM STATEMENT

India’s temporary workforce is trapped in predatory, informal hiring chaos.

Across urban commercial centers and college hubs nationwide, India’s ₹2.4 Lakh Crore temporary workforce operates almost entirely through informal WhatsApp groups, unregulated commission middlemen, and word-of-mouth. For students and gig workers, shifts are plagued by wage cuts and ghosted applications. For businesses, sudden no-shows destroy peak retail revenue.

Workforce Community Mascot · Student & Retail Gig Worker · Pan-India Field Observation

“Whether you’re in Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, or Kolkata, every gig worker has the exact same nightmare: waking up to an unverified WhatsApp shift, commuting 18 km, and having the floor supervisor say ‘sorry, someone else arrived first’.”

Contract 01

No Hex in Components

Components never consume raw hex codes directly. Every visual property resolves through semantic token aliases, eliminating hardcoded styling drift.

Contract 02

State is Explicit

Every interactive element features dedicated tokens for default, hover/pressed, focus, and disabled states.

Contract 03

Soft + Strong Pairing

Status badges pair low-opacity background fills (e.g. green 100) with high-contrast text and indicator pips (green 700) for instant glanceability.

Contract 04

Color is Redundant

Color never carries meaning alone. Every critical status pairs tint with explicit typographic labels, icons, or tactile shapes to guarantee accessibility.

16 / 18 · USABILITY TESTING & ITERATIONS

Field usability: 5 critical issues and design iterations

Rather than relying on aesthetic assumptions, every core flow underwent usability scrutiny across field test cohorts. Here are 5 user friction points uncovered during testing and the tested design iterations engineered to resolve them.

Friction 01 · Onboarding drop-off Fix implemented

Heavy upfront identity gating

Testing discovery: Requiring full Aadhaar upload and bank KYC before users could view shift feeds caused severe first-session abandonment.

Design iteration: Shifted to progressive disclosure. Users browse and filter shifts freely; document verification is deferred until the final "Commit to Shift" step.

V1 Initial Flow Mandatory upfront Aadhaar & bank upload blocked shift browse → 64% first-session drop-off.
V2 Tested Resolution Progressive discovery: free browse & filter; KYC deferred until shift commitment → 89% completion.
Friction 02 · Trust & legitimacy Fix implemented

Anxiety over unverified listings

Testing discovery: Workers hesitated to tap apply because informal platforms frequently post fraudulent or locked-gate listings.

Design iteration: Introduced prominent "Verified Business" badges, physical venue addresses, and direct supervisor contact cards before commitment.

V1 Initial Flow Generic employer names without venue photos or supervisor contacts sparked scam hesitation.
V2 Tested Resolution Physical store address, venue manager badge, and locked escrow guarantee displayed upfront.
Friction 03 · Information hierarchy Fix implemented

Buried pay rates and commute distance

Testing discovery: Early cards placed job descriptions at the top and wages in fine print, forcing users to open every card just to check net pay and travel time.

Design iteration: Redesigned card anatomy to anchor guaranteed payout in the top right, paired with real-time transit calculation ("22 min away").

V1 Initial Flow Pay rate buried in 12pt body copy; transit distance required switching to Google Maps.
V2 Tested Resolution ₹5,000 tabular wage banner anchored top right + real-time GPS transit calculation ("22 min away").
Friction 04 · Ghosting anxiety Fix implemented

Post-application limbo and uncertainty

Testing discovery: In 42.9% of typical gig cases, applicants face radio silence. Users did not know if submissions were received or reviewed.

Design iteration: Engineered a 7-stage live status tracker in 'My Shifts' with explicit 19-minute SLA review timers and alternative recommendations if not selected.

V1 Initial Flow Static "Application submitted" card with zero updates or time estimate → high anxiety.
V2 Tested Resolution 7-Stage live status stepper with live 19-minute SLA countdown timer & push updates.
Friction 05 · Supervisor latency Fix implemented

Venue arrival attendance bottleneck

Testing discovery: Workers waiting at venue gates risked being marked absent when busy store managers were unavailable to scan QR codes on schedule.

Design iteration: Engineered a geofenced self-arrival ping with encrypted timestamp logging that preserves worker punctuality even prior to physical manager sign-off.

V1 Initial Flow Attendance required manager to physically scan worker screen; caused shift start delays.
V2 Tested Resolution Dual verification: GPS geofence self-check-in pings store server with encrypted timestamp.
