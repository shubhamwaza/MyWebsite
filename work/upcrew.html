01 The Problem

Informal hiring costs trust on both sides

India's gig workforce is largely managed through WhatsApp groups and referral chains — with no verification layer, no dispute mechanism, and no record of who showed up.

01
No verification layer

Businesses hire from word-of-mouth. Workers show up without documented history or accountability.

02
Legitimate vs. exploitative

Workers can't distinguish real opportunities from bad-faith postings. Trust is absent from both sides.

03
Scheduling chaos

No-shows, last-minute cancellations, and pay disputes with no paper trail to resolve them.
