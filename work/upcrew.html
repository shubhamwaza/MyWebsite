THE PRODUCT & THE CHALLENGE

Flexible work shouldn’t mean uncertain decisions.

PRODUCT CONTEXT

A worker–side shift companion

UpCrew brings nearby opportunities, applications, upcoming shifts, earnings and account details into one mobile experience. The supplied screens show retail, hospitality, event and warehouse roles around Thane and Mumbai.

DESIGN CHALLENGE

Make the next step understandable

The interface needs to explain what a shift pays, when and where it happens, whether an application has been accepted, and what to do before arrival. Those questions change as the worker moves through the journey.

PRINCIPLE / CLARITY

Facts before commitment

Keep pay, schedule and location together so the choice is informed, not just visually attractive.

PRINCIPLE / CONTINUITY

State before action

Distinguish sending an application from being selected. Let each status explain what happens next.

PRINCIPLE / AGENCY

Help without a dead end

Permission deferral, code recovery and visible support preserve a route forward.

Contract 01

No Hex in Components

Components never consume raw hex codes directly. Every visual property resolves through semantic token aliases, eliminating hardcoded styling drift.

Contract 02

State is Explicit

Every interactive element features dedicated tokens for default, hover/pressed, focus, and disabled states.

Contract 03

Soft + Strong Pairing

Status badges pair low-opacity background fills (e.g. green 100) with high-contrast text and indicator pips (green 700) for instant glanceability.

Contract 04

Color is Redundant

Color never carries meaning alone. Every critical status pairs tint with explicit typographic labels, icons, or tactile shapes to guarantee accessibility.

PERMISSIONS & CONTEXTUAL ACCESS

Ask when needed. Explain why it matters.

Location and notification permissions are deferred until the user understands their utility. Clear rationale and zero penalties for skipping keep agency in the worker's hands.

Value before access

Contextual prompts explain that notification permissions unlock instant shift alerts and location accuracy reduces travel uncertainty before triggering system-level dialogs.

Granular, independent choice

Notifications and location services operate independently. Workers can enable push notifications for shift releases while keeping geolocation manual.

Zero-friction deferral

"Not now" and "Skip" remain prominent and accessible with zero penalty. Workers can freely browse all shifts across Mumbai and Thane without granting immediate access.

DESIGNING FOR REALITY / EDGE CASES

Field resilience: zero dead ends in the real world.

In day-to-day gig work, users encounter unstable cellular networks, empty search radius states, document rejections, and employer schedule changes. Every edge state provides an immediate, actionable escape route.

Empty States

Zero Shifts in Selected Radius

Never a dead end. Explains why 0 results matched, allows 1-tap expansion from 5 km to 15 km, and offers push notifications for nearby openings.

Verification States

Document Rejection & Recovery

Avoids vague rejection banners. Highlights the exact document issue (such as glare or unreadable text) with clear instructions for re-capture.

Offline Resilience

Offline Confirmed Shift Pass

Caches confirmed shift location, supervisor contact details, and venue directions locally so workers retain access even if mobile reception drops in transit.

Cancellation UX

Employer Emergency Cancellation

Provides immediate notice with guaranteed cancellation compensation details (₹500 payout) and proactive recommendations for alternative shifts in the same time window.

Reliability Tracking

Verified On-Site Live Check-In

Protects attendance and reliability records through on-site supervisor confirmation and verified location coordinates.

Timers & Urgency

3-Hour Transit & Shift Reminder

Proactive countdown notifications with estimated transit times and supervisor details to prevent accidental delays or no-shows.

RETROSPECTIVE & STRATEGIC ROADMAP

Designing for human dignity at scale.

Key strategic takeaways from codifying a two-sided gig labor marketplace and architectural milestones for taking UpCrew toward production.

Pillar 01 · Trust Metric

Quantifying Trust in User Testing

While qualitative interviews confirmed the necessity of verification, future testing should include structured Likert-scale evaluations to measure perceived security across verified and unverified listings.

Pillar 02 · Market Liquidity

Cold-Start Supply and Demand

Two-sided marketplaces face liquidity challenges early on. In future iterations, model hyper-localized pilot rollouts targeted at high-density event crews and college campuses in Thane and Mumbai.

Pillar 03 · Algorithmic Fairness

Phase 2: Predictive Shift Matching

Moving from manual shift browsing to smart algorithmic recommendations based on recurring worker availability, preferred transit corridors, and historical reliability ratings.

Pillar 04 · Financial Infrastructure

Phase 3: Digital Escrow Settlement

Automating fund reservation upon shift creation and releasing direct UPI payouts immediately after verified supervisor checkout, eliminating payment disputes entirely.
