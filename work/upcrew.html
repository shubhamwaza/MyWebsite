01 The Problem

Informal hiring costs trust on both sides of the market

India's gig workforce runs on WhatsApp groups and referral chains — with no identity layer, no dispute path, and no record of who actually showed up.

01
No verification layer

Businesses hire from word-of-mouth. Workers have no documented history or accountability trail.

02
No signal of legitimacy

Workers can't distinguish real opportunities from exploitative postings — trust is absent from both sides.

03
No system for resolution

No-shows, cancellations, and pay disputes happen with no paper trail and no path to resolve them.
