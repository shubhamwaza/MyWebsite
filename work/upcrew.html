The Strategic Shift

A simpler onboarding unlocked 2× more completed sign-ups

Mid-project usability testing revealed that a comprehensive 8-step onboarding was causing 60% drop-off before profile completion.

Restructured into 3-step progressive disclosure — collect only essentials upfront, defer verification and preferences to post-first-action moments.
Scrapped

Core Interaction

Reducing decision fatigue in shift selection

User Dilemma

Workers see 40+ shifts with no way to prioritize by fit, distance, or pay.

Design Solution

Smart filter chips + saved preference presets reduce visible options to 5–8 relevant matches per session.
