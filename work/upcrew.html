The Friction

Informal hiring costs businesses time, trust, and reliable workers

  • Hiring relies on WhatsApp groups and word-of-mouth — no verification, no accountability.
  • Workers can't distinguish legitimate opportunities from exploitative ones.
  • Both sides waste hours on scheduling, no-shows, and miscommunication.
